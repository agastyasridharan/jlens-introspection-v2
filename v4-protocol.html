J-lens introspection · Protocol v4

Introspection v4: Cued single-token hold

Qwen3.6-27B · J++ lens · October 2026 Pilot done · no-go

The v2 copy-and-hold prompt, with the hold pinned to one named word of the sentence. The pilot tests whether the lens shows the chosen item concentrated at that word. Results are on the v4 results page.

Model: Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B (thinking disabled, greedy decoding). Lens: J++ (koayon/jpp-lenses), as in v2/v3.

We want to answer this: if the model is told to think of its chosen item only while it writes one named word of the copied sentence, does the lens show the item concentrated at that word? If so, does the model's report of the item read the content at that word?

v4 keeps the v2 prompt almost word for word and pins the hold to one cue word:

Prompt

Imagine {setting}. Silently choose one {category phrase} suitable for that setting. Copy the sentence below exactly as written, word for word, and write nothing else. Think about the {unit} you chose only while you write the word "{cue}", and not while writing any other word. Do not name or describe the {unit}.

Sentence: {carrier}

{unit} is "item" for groceries, "animal", "country".

What changed from v2 and v3

Earlier v4 Why
v2: the lens is read at every copied word and averaged; about 5 different lens winners per trial. One readout position, fixed in advance: the cue word as it is copied. One pre-registered position, so no winner has to be picked among positions.
v3: the single readout token (assistant start) is one the model had no reason to use. The model is told to put the item at the readout position. The content should be concentrated where we read.
v3: a prompt-state swap never reached the report; only a late edit did. The swap is at the cue word, with an off-cue swap at another word as the spatial control. A report that reads the cued moment should follow the cue swap more than the off-cue swap. Late steering cannot produce that pattern.

0. Judge and lens scoring

Unchanged from v2: gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17, frozen v2 prompts and label cache (new tokens are labeled with the same prompts). Readout block 54 and K = 10 are carried over from v2 and frozen. The lens score of an item at one position is its best log-probability among top-100 tokens labeled as that item (floor: rank-100 log-probability); the corrected score subtracts the same item's score at the same position of the no-instruction copy of the same carrier (v2 baselines b00–b11).

1. Pilot (this stage)

Situations. The 24 v2 preliminary situations (8 per category), each with its v2 carrier. Each carrier gets three cue words, early, middle and late in the sentence; every situation is run once per cue (72 cued trials). Cue words are single tokens in the carrier, content words with no obvious link to the categories, and frozen before generation (v4/stimuli/cues.json).

Comparisons that already exist. For the same 24 situations, v2's hold-all trial (identical setting and carrier, hold over the whole copy) and the no-instruction copy of each carrier.

Generation and recording. Greedy, up to 64 tokens; valid only if the copy is exact. One forward pass over prompt plus copy; J++ top 100 at every block and at every position from the start of the user message to the end of the copy.

Report (descriptive in the pilot). In a separate branch: "Which {unit} were you thinking about while you wrote "{cue}"? Reply with its name." (16 tokens), and the v2 condition-2 question for comparison.

Measures (block 54; per copied token position p):

  • Item content M(p): the number of distinct judge-labeled category Items in the lens top 100 at p (v2's readout-block metric). Secondary: the summed lens probability of Item-labeled tokens.
  • Cue effect (primary): for the cue word w of a trial, M(w) when w is cued minus the mean M(w) in the other two cue variants of the same situation (the same word, same setting, cued elsewhere). This compares a position with itself, so it is not biased by position or by picking a winner.
  • Peak at cue: the share of trials where the cue token has the largest M among the carrier's copied tokens (excluding the final period). Chance is about 1/10.
  • v2 comparison: M(w) cued minus M(w) in v2's hold-all trial of the same situation.
  • Position sensitivity: the same measures at the token before the cue (where the cue is predicted).
  • Leak into the prompt: M at the cue word inside the instruction ("…the word "{cue}"…") and at the cue word in the prompt's Sentence line, compared with the copied cue token.
  • Eligibility: the reference item (top corrected Item at the cue token) is in the top K = 10 there. Reported alongside v2 and v3 rates.
  • Per-block curve of the cue effect, blocks 0–62 (descriptive).

Go / no-go for the causal stage (frozen before the pilot runs). Proceed to the main and swap stages only if, pooled over categories, (a) the cue effect at block 54 is positive with a bootstrap 95% lower bound above 0 (resampling situations), and (b) the peak-at-cue rate is at least three times chance (≥ 30%). Otherwise we stop and report the pilot: the instruction does not localize lens content.

2. Main and swap stages (only if the pilot passes; to be frozen after the pilot)

Sketch, to be fixed in an amendment before any main data: new settings per category; one cue per trial, position balanced; reference item A at the cue token and runner-up B; swap A↔B at the cue token (blocks 48–53, as in v3) vs the same swap at a non-cued word of matched distance ("off-cue") vs a norm-matched random edit at the cue; copy must stay identical; report question as above plus recognition; late edit and other-instance prediction carried over from v3.

Addendum (written after the pilot, 2026-11-03)

The pilot failed both frozen criteria: the cue effect at the copied cue token was −0.62 items (95% CI −1.38 to +0.00), and the cue token was the peak in 0 of 72 trials. Per the rule above, the main and swap stages are not run. The measure listed under position sensitivity (the token before the cue) showed a large effect; it is reported on the results page as exploratory. Any follow-up that uses that position needs a new pre-registered protocol.
