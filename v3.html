Introspection v3: Natural-recommendation swap

Qwen3.6-27B · J++ lens · October 2026 Preliminary stage done

The model answers an ordinary recommendation request; we read the lens just before it answers, find the other items represented there (competitors), and later ask the model about them, swapping two competitors without changing its answer. Design: protocol v3.

Summary so far (steps 0–4)
  • Readout block 53, K = 10. Block 53 has the most category items at the assistant-start token (the same block the earlier natural pilot chose). At K = 10, 21/24 preliminary instrument situations have at least two competitors besides the model’s answer, so no fallback step was needed.
  • Categories kept: instruments (21/24 eligible) and countries (24/24). Animals miss the screen by one situation (11/24) and groceries fail it (0/24); both are dropped from the swap stage. The protocol allows one replacement category.
  • The model’s answer is usually not what the lens shows most. Most instrument answers (ukulele, keyboard, cello, cajón) never appear in the lens top 100 at block 53; the competitors are common instruments (guitar, violin, flute, piano, guqin). For countries the model answers Japan in 20/24 situations, with competitors such as Canada, the United States and the United Kingdom.
  • Judge validated for the new work: 200/200 random instrument token labels and 50/50 answer-item lists correct; rerun identity 142/144 tokens and 50/50 answers. Three item-level problems found in the non-None labels were fixed as dated amendments; they change no eligibility count.
  • Next: step 6 development (edit-setting grid and manipulation check) on new situations. No reports have been collected.
