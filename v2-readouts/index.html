    Readout

    J++ lens (koayon/jpp-lenses, qwen3.6-27b), top 20 tokens by log-probability at blocks 40–54. Tokens with no letter or digit are removed before ranking; nothing else is filtered. Category items are the frozen judge labels (gpt-5.4-mini) with the v2 synonym map.

    Reference

    Each item’s score is its best-token log-probability averaged over the copied tokens (floored at the rank-100 value), minus the same score on the baseline trial with the same sentence. The reference A is the top corrected item; a trial is eligible when A reaches the top K = 10 at some copied token.

    Edits

    Swap: the A and B lens coordinates are exchanged at block 54 on every copied token (α = 1 exchanges, α = 2 overshoots). Control: a random direction orthogonal to both, matched in norm. Valid when the copy is unchanged.

    Exactness

    Token strings are the saved prompt and copy ids, decoded one token at a time. Nothing is regenerated on this page.
