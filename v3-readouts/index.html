    Readout

    J++ lens (koayon/jpp-lenses, qwen3.6-27b) top 20 tokens by log-probability at blocks 40–54, excluding tokens with no letter or digit. Category items are the frozen judge labels (gpt-5.4-mini) with the v3 synonym map. The v3 readout block is 53 and K = 10.

    Edits

    Swap: the B and C lens coordinates are exchanged at α = 4 over blocks 48–53 on the last five prompt tokens. Control: a random direction orthogonal to both, matched in norm. Δ = log p(C) − log p(B) at the position just before the answer item, with the answer teacher-forced.

    Reports

    Report tags: C and B are the two competitors; X means the report named an item the answer already named. Labels are the frozen v3 judge labels; v5 reports come from the bf16 rerun with the original answer teacher-forced.

    Exactness

    Token strings are the saved prompt and answer ids, decoded one token at a time. Nothing is regenerated on this page.
