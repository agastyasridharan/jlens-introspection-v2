Introspection v2: Results

Qwen3.6-27B · J++ lens · October 2026 Updating live

Qwen3.6-27B is asked to silently choose a grocery item, animal, or country for a setting and keep it in mind while copying a neutral sentence. We read the J++ lens at every copied token, then ask the model (in separate branches) which item it was keeping in mind. Design: protocol v2. Step 8 (the causal swap) is deliberately not run here.

2Hold phase: copy validity

All 516 hold-phase trials ran on 8 H200s in FP32 (one replica per GPU, eager attention, TF32 off, greedy decoding, thinking disabled). Every reply reproduced the carrier exactly, so no trial is lost to the validity check. Each trial was recorded at every block output (0–62) at each copied token and at the assistant-start token; the J++ lens top 100 (tokens with no letter or digit excluded, log-softmax over the remaining vocabulary) was saved at every block and position.

Copy validity. Valid = reply, whitespace stripped, equals the carrier, then end of turn.

Appendix

Implementation notes and deviations
  • Judge model. gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17, reasoning off, temperature 0, strict JSON schema, one input per call (the judge never sees other tokens, the trial, or the setting). We first tried gpt-5.4-nano; on 25 hand-made probe tokens it labeled incomplete words such as “pomegran” and “banan” as Items (4/25 errors vs 1/25 for mini, the one being a Fragment), so we switched before labeling any data.
  • Lens log-probabilities are a log-softmax over the vocabulary after removing tokens with no letter or digit (the J++ readout filter), so baseline correction compares like with like.
  • Hold-phase and free-naming generation ran for all 516 trials in one pass, before the readout block and K were fixed. Nothing in the hold phase or free naming depends on the block or K, and the main-trial lens data are not analyzed until both are frozen from the preliminary trials. Recognition (which needs the readout block) runs afterwards from the saved post-hold state.
  • Settings were generated with gpt-5.5 (5 thematic batches of 60 per category), screened for naming any specific item, food, animal, country, city, nationality or language, screened for category fit, deduplicated against each other and the preliminary settings, and 160 per category drawn with a fixed seed. Frozen before any hold-phase trial.
  • Carriers are balanced: each of the 12 carriers is used 40 times in the main experiment and twice in the preliminary trials.
  • Hand-checks of judge labels (step 0 validation) are done by Claude, not by a human; the sampled labels are listed on this page so they can be audited.
