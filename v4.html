Pilot result
  • Pre-registered result: no-go. At the copied cue word, cueing does not raise lens item content: cue effect -0.62 items (95% CI -1.38 to +0.00, 24 situations), and the cue word is the peak in 0% of trials (chance 9%). Both frozen criteria fail, so the main and swap stages were not run.
  • The content moves one token earlier. At the token before the cue (the position where the model is about to write the cue word), cueing adds +18.1 items (CI +13.8 to +22.9), and that token is the peak in 56% of trials. Exploratory: this position was a listed sensitivity check, not the primary readout.
  • It is mostly category content, not the chosen item. The top lens items there are prototypes (“dog” in 13 of 24 animal trials; “apple” or “bread” in 13 of 24 grocery trials). The model's report names the top lens item in 6% of trials. The report is among the top 5 lens items 31% of the time, against 21% for a report from another situation.
  • The same content appears when the instruction itself names the cue. At the opening quote before the cue word in the instruction (“…the word "”), there are 31.6 items on average, against 25.3 at the copied token before the cue. This suggests the effect is tied to the cue string, not to a held choice.

1Design

The v2 copy-and-hold prompt, with one added sentence that pins the hold to a named word of the sentence. 24 v2 preliminary situations (8 per category, same settings and carriers as v2) × 3 cue words (early, middle, late) = 72 trials. Greedy decoding, thinking disabled; the J++ lens top 100 at every block and every position from the user message to the end of the copy.

Example prompt (p-grocery-000-early):

Imagine a birthday breakfast. Silently choose one item commonly bought at a grocery store suitable for that setting. Copy the sentence below exactly as written, word for word, and write nothing else. Think about the item you chose only while you write the word "painting", and not while writing any other word. Do not name or describe the item.

Sentence: The old painting hung crookedly on the wall.

Report questions, each in its own branch after the copy:

Which item were you thinking about while you wrote "painting"? Reply with its name.
While you were copying that sentence, which item commonly bought at a grocery store were you keeping in mind? Reply with the name of that item.

Main measure, at readout block 54 (frozen from v2): M(p), the number of distinct judge-labeled items in the lens top 100 at position p. The cue effect compares one word with itself: M at word w when w is the cue, minus the mean M at the same word when one of the other two words is the cue (same setting and carrier). Go criterion (frozen): pooled cue effect with bootstrap 95% lower bound above 0, and cue word the peak in at least 30% of trials.

5Decision

Per the frozen rule, v4 stops at the pilot. The instruction does not put the chosen item at the cue word. It does make category content appear just before the cue word is written, and also where the instruction quotes the cue. That content is mostly generic, and the reports do not track it. A swap at the token before the cue would edit content the report does not appear to read, so a follow-up there would need its own pre-registered protocol and a case for why the report should depend on it.
